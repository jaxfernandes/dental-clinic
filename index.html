Safety protocols

Hospital grade infection control, followed on every single patient.

  • Class B autoclave sterilisationEvery instrument is vacuum sterilised at 134 degrees and verified with indicator strips.
  • Sealed & dated pouchesSterile instruments stay sealed in dated pouches and are opened only in front of you.
  • Single use disposablesNeedles, suction tips, gloves, masks and glasses are used once and never reused.
  • Full surface disinfectionThe chair, lights and handles are wiped with hospital disinfectant between every patient.
  • Clean water linesDental unit water lines are flushed and treated daily so nothing travels back to you.
  • Full PPE for every procedureGloves, mask, face shield and apron changed between patients, without exception.

Emergency care

Dental emergency after hours?

The clinic is open 9 AM to 4 PM, Monday to Saturday. If something happens outside those hours, do exactly this.

Tooth knocked out

  1. Hold it by the crown, never the root.
  2. Rinse gently with milk or water. Do not scrub.
  3. Try to place it back in the socket, or store it in milk.
  4. Reach a dentist within 60 minutes. Time decides everything.

Severe tooth pain

  1. Rinse with warm salt water.
  2. Hold a cold compress on your cheek for 15 minutes.
  3. Take your usual pain relief if you have it.
  4. Never place aspirin directly on the gum. It burns.

Broken or chipped tooth

  1. Save every piece you can find and keep it in milk.
  2. Avoid hot, cold and sweet food on that side.
  3. Cover any sharp edge with sugarless chewing gum.
  4. Message us a photo on WhatsApp first thing in the morning.

Facial swelling

  1. Swelling with a toothache usually means infection.
  2. Apply cold, never heat, on the outside.
  3. Call or WhatsApp us immediately for advice.
  4. If you have fever or trouble swallowing, go to the nearest hospital at once.
Call the clinic now WhatsApp the doctor
If swelling is spreading fast, or there is difficulty in breathing, swallowing or opening the mouth, do not wait for us. Go straight to the nearest hospital emergency room.
