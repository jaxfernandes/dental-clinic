Your doctor

Care that begins with listening

Dr. Krishna Mohana believes a trip to the dentist should never be something to fear. Her clinic blends modern equipment with a slow, unhurried approach — explaining every step before it happens, and never recommending a treatment you don't truly need.

From a child's very first check-up to a grandparent's new dentures, every patient gets the same promise: honest advice, gentle hands, and a chair that never feels scary.
